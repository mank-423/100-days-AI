
MEIOSIS - BIOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Meiosis is essential for sexual reproduction.

Its major functions include:

1. Chromosome Number Maintenance:
   Meiosis produces haploid gametes. When fertilization occurs, two haploid gametes combine to restore the diploid chromosome number.

2. Genetic Variation Through Crossing Over:
   During Prophase I, homologous chromosomes exchange genetic material. This creates new combinations of alleles.

3. Independent Assortment:
   Homologous chromosome pairs align randomly during Metaphase I. This produces different combinations of maternal and paternal chromosomes.

4. Evolution:
   Genetic variation produced by meiosis contributes to variation within populations and provides material for natural selection.

5. Comparison with Mitosis:
   Mitosis generally produces two genetically similar daughter cells.
   Meiosis generally produces four genetically different haploid cells.
