
MEIOSIS - BASIC OVERVIEW

Meiosis is a type of cell division that produces four haploid cells from one diploid parent cell.

A diploid cell contains two sets of chromosomes (2n), while a haploid cell contains one set (n).

In humans:
- Most body cells contain 46 chromosomes.
- Human gametes (sperm and egg) contain 23 chromosomes.
- Meiosis reduces the chromosome number from 46 to 23.

Meiosis consists of two successive divisions:
1. Meiosis I: Homologous chromosomes separate.
2. Meiosis II: Sister chromatids separate.

Unlike mitosis, meiosis produces genetically varied cells.
