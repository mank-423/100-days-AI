
MEIOSIS - PROCESS AND STAGES

Meiosis takes place through two divisions.

MEIOSIS I (Reductional Division)

1. Prophase I:
   - Homologous chromosomes pair up.
   - Crossing over occurs between non-sister chromatids.
   - Genetic material is exchanged.

2. Metaphase I:
   - Homologous chromosome pairs align at the cell's equator.
   - Their orientation is random.

3. Anaphase I:
   - Homologous chromosomes move to opposite poles.
   - Sister chromatids remain attached.

4. Telophase I:
   - The cell divides into two haploid cells.

MEIOSIS II (Equational Division)

1. Prophase II: Chromosomes condense.
2. Metaphase II: Chromosomes align individually.
3. Anaphase II: Sister chromatids separate.
4. Telophase II: Cells divide.

The final result is usually four haploid cells.
